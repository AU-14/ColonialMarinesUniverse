# Garrison small-prop supports

Five additional draft models and one revised gray rack. These complete the specific tables and rack variants beneath the newly authored supplies. All remain drafts.

New geometric work is contributed under CC0-1.0 to the extent separately licensable. Source visual-design and derivative rights retain their existing licenses and attribution.

## Placement

Prison and reinforced tables provide a full tile support surface at Z=.86. Open racks provide their actual upper deck at Z=.948, using its .8 by .55 tile footprint. The placement resolver checks the saved prop pivot against that deck; adjacent floor props are not lifted automatically. Table edge connections and arbitrary overlapping loose items are still unresolved. The two tinted racks and Soro prison table use explicit referenceTint/bakedSpriteTint so the native scene does not multiply their tint twice.
