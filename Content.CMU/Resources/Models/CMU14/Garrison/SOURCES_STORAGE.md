# Garrison storage and furnishings sources

26 draft solid assemblies. Source sprites, default layering, saved positions and available fixtures were inspected. Geometry is authored, not generated by extruding sprite pixels.

New geometric work is contributed under CC0-1.0 to the extent separately licensable. Existing source visual-design and derivative rights retain their original licenses and attribution.

## Placement and fidelity

Dumpsters span two tiles around their original left-tile pivot (X -0.5..+1.5); their 128px sprite padding is not copied as a ground shift. Directional benches and stools preserve their saved facing and asymmetric ends. Narrow closets/cabinets use fixed front artwork and collider-informed widths. Shelf stock is modeled as separate packages in seven distinct arrangements. Pallet spacer gaps remain open. The later placement correction declares five distinct highest-board labels through `supportSurfaces` on each pallet model. Board gaps remain unsupported; no hidden solid deck is added. Top heights are .15/.621 tiles; .0015-tile grain relief stays below the .002 prop clearance. Chair #1719 and rack parts #14400 now rest on their actual pallet boards. See `SOURCES_FOLDED_EQUIPMENT.md`.

Source default layers are not always a rendered runtime state. BaseBagOpenClose hides openLayer when closed; dumpsters and filing cabinets here use only their closed reference. Closets use their closed door state. Opening, welding, contents and custom labels remain unfinished. Upholstered bench comparisons show the tinted cover layer; the frame was reviewed separately in the composite. Pallet RSI metadata declares four directions, but East/West frames are empty; those model views are inferred. Heights, unseen backs, depth and round profiles remain approximations. No draft is marked reviewed.
