# Xeno structures: source and authoring notes

92 authored solid assemblies cover 104 concrete hive prototypes. The four existing maintenance-cover variants are retained (the unbound maintenance hatch variant now has an exact binding). Four transient construction effects remain native sprites. All living mobs and parasites remain sprites. The audit includes every non-mob descendant of the RMC and CMU Xeno structure prototype directories; invisible collision helpers are outside the visual inventory.

Geometry follows the source families: ordinary/thick/queen/weedbound/reflective resin and membranes; mycelium variants; doors; eggs and debris; nests; floor resin, spikes and traps; weed nodes and wall growth; tunnels; fruits; hive cores, clusters, pylons, recovery/plasma organs, acid pillars, egg morphers, king cocoons and pathogen spore organs. Depth and hidden faces are authored interpretations of 2D art. All assets remain drafts pending a live playthrough and art acceptance.

The opt-in 3D renderer samples the existing visible RSI layers and animation frames. Door leaves retract during opening/closing, egg shells open and break, fruit uses its item/growing/grown/spent state, weed roots follow cardinal connection states, and ghost-only markers are included only when the client actually sees them. Hidden layers are not made visible. Unknown appearances retain the original sprite. No new gameplay timing, server behavior, mobs, AI or attachment system is introduced. Static weeds do not get queued for animation updates. The existing optional-library lifecycle applies to the new geometry.

Walls tile to full tile boundaries; membranes use translucent closed volumes. Nests and wall growth are static solids. Construction nodes have static preview bodies and a modeled bud; the original HUD glyph is not reconstructed as text. Alternate legacy hive sprites and unmodeled shader/transform states fall back to native sprites. Underground tunnel travel stays game-owned: the model depicts the rim and dark entrance, not an invented underground map. Saved offline map previews remain snapshots; GLBs contain a default composed scene plus separately inspectable source-layer frame scenes, without a second animation clock.

Regenerate with `python Tools/three_d/author_xeno_structures.py`. Export its YAML to a staging directory with `build_models.py --source ... --no-review`, then merge the resulting files and manifest entries into the complete library. Do not run a subset export directly into the full asset folder. Review sheets compare source art with front, rear and underside renders. No texture-atlas slots were added.
