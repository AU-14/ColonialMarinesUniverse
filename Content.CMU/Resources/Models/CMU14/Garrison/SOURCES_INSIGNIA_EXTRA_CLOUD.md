# Loose insignia and medical accessory art drafts

Source checkpoint: TheHellFireo/CMU-Garrison-3D, Chip/garrison-3d, commit 6e37a4d0a7d9433838c393a82c02422cb704dd5a.

16 editable assemblies bind 16 previously unmapped Redux physical prototypes (17 inventory records). All remain draft.

## Construction and scope
The eleven patches are genuinely thin printed/embroidered objects. Their original face art is preserved without resampling on a separate shallow face, backed by solid pieces following the exact opaque pixel contour. A 32×32 point audit for each patch confirms no rectangular backing fills its transparent perimeter. The plain colored reverse, thickness and pixel-stepped cloth edge are inferred. This is not cloth simulation or a smooth embroidery reconstruction.

Three armbands have real open elliptical loops and connected sewn upper lips, with source-colored sectors. Their flattened loop interpretation, cross-section and unseen rear coloring are inferred. The medallion has a solid contour-shaped metal body, separate colored ribbon and two thin suspension legs; eight source icon frames preserve the 12-second dwell followed by seven 0.1-second glint frames. The portable clip is not a native runtime test. The tourniquet has a real loop, buckle, red tab, support bridge and independent windlass rod. Applying, tightening, removing or refunding it is unsupported behavior.

## Source and state mapping
All bindings target the loose world icon. No equipped/helmet/held sprite is substituted. The source-owned accessory attachment categories, player sprite layers, turning and character deformation retain their original behavior; no rig or body fixture is authored. Source scale is the sprite pixel extent at 32 pixels per tile; physical depth is inferred. Additional prototype appearance offsets/instance overrides, native lighting, support surfaces, map placement contacts and gameplay remain unverified.

## Verification
40 source files match their pinned Git blob hashes. All20 original-detail crops retain exact RGBA pixels; untextured paint colors are taken from source palettes. Unchanged canonical exporter output is deterministic; finite positive parts and budget pass. Default-visible GLB convex hull checks prove positive-volume connections among the modeled structural solids; four loop cavities are sampled separately. Textured-face bounding hulls are conservative and do not claim alpha-shaped collision. Blender import is reported separately. Khronos glTF Validator has not been run. No native, whole-model manifold, game physics, full-map, worn-pose or fidelity approval is claimed.
