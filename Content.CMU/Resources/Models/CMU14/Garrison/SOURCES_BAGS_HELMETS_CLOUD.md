## Default state and inherited appearance inspection

- AU14LACNAmmoBackpack, AU14SlingSatchelBlack, CMSatchel and RMCSatchelDemoSpec inherit CMPersonalStorageBase layers icon, openLayer and closedLayer. The inherited storage container starts empty. CMStorageVisualizer.ShowOpenClosedWhenEmpty defaults false, and CMStorageVisualizerSystem explicitly hides open and closed layers when StorageUsed == 0. These models therefore use icon only, not all three visible YAML layers composited together. Open/closed PNGs were fetched and inspected but are not modeled or bound. This proves the chosen source default, not that each saved Redux entity is empty.
- The ammo backpack icon and closed PNGs have identical Git blob hashes. Open differs in the lower bag region; the attached ammunition can remains shut. The low physical can, lid and straps do not invent an open ammo box.
- The two satchels have no raised top handle in their empty icon. Raised handles and changed flap positions in filled open/closed overlays are deliberately not imported into this default. The inferred compact rear sling lies beneath the bag and is joined at the sides.
- The rocket bag icon has collapsed low sleeves. Closed and open overlays extend the paired silos. No protruding filled tubes, rockets or contents are inferred in the default.
- AU14TentBigFoldedSurgical explicitly selects base_tent plus med_base. It does not select big_tent despite its ID/name. The separate blue medical-cross pixels are preserved unchanged. Deployment definitions are read-only reference; deployed tent geometry, deployed facilities, transition and rig are excluded.
- ClothingBackpackDebug4 changes the Storage grid and inherits the standard grey backpack icon unchanged. It has no source open/closed layer visualizer. The peculiar storage layout does not justify inventing different geometry.
- AU14HeadHelmetRMCMedic and AU14LACNHelmetSL inherit RMCMarineHelmetBase. The medic begins with an integrated visor toggled, but VisorSystem supplies its layer via GetEquipmentVisualsEvent for the equipped wearer. These loose models keep the ground icon. Dynamic squad tint, helmet accessories, stains, visor-equipped layers and worn/held forms remain unsupported.
- RMCArmorHelmetSPPFirefighter inherits Foldable and FoldableClothing through its helmet ancestry. The inspected foldable definition changes hidden hair layers; the source RSI has icon and equipped-HELMET only. No alternate world icon, folded partner or state binding is invented.
- RMCHelmetGarbWire has an independent loose icon and a four-direction helmet accessory state. Only its loose ring is modeled. It is not attached to any helmet model in this batch.

## Physical interpretation and limitations

The three helmets have real open undersides, connected wall/brim rings and twelve joined pitched roof facets. A small connected crown cap closes the top. The crown seams, curved brim approximation and shallow conical profile remain visible and explicitly draft. The supported primitive contract cannot express a smooth thin concave dome; these are not fidelity-approved helmet shells.

Satchels use a recessed floor, physical body panels, side gussets and mouth lips. The rocket sleeves have open mouth regions, a common carrier and a lower pouch. Backpack bodies have finite depth with attached pockets, flaps and retaining straps. The folded tent has separate compressed rolls and cloth folds. The wire ring is built from connected rod segments with filed nubs. No model uses a whole-sprite slab. Source textures are only 1–30-pixel detail crops; hidden depth, underside construction and absolute thickness remain inferred.

Independent convex-hull checks on the actual exported GLB vertices establish one positive-volume contact component for every model. Eight selected cavity samples have unobstructed paths from below or above: three helmet interiors, two satchel slits, two rocket sleeve mouths and the wire-loop center. This is a bounded sample/contact proof, not whole-mesh manifoldness, sewn cloth topology or full-volume watertightness.

Only static loose ground/world studies are supplied. No worn, held, actor, rigged, runtime open/closed/fill, tint, damage, accessory or deployed-tent coverage is claimed. No saved-map placement, collision clearance, native admission, performance, Khronos validation or fidelity approval is claimed. Historical inventory lists ten Redux and seven classic records for these IDs; those are potential reference counts only.

## Source ownership and reproducible checks

`reference/bags-helmets-inventory.json` records the original queue inventory. `reference/bags-helmets-fetches.json` contains exact upstream paths, Git blob SHA-1s and pinned source URLs for 45 fetched files. `reference/bags-helmets-source/` preserves all those exact source blobs, including owner YAML, metadata, icons, optional bag states, inherited storage and helmet definitions and relevant C# visualizer/visor owner code. Source files are evidence only; they are not modified runtime files.

Run from the repository root:

1. `python Tools/three_d/author_bags_helmets_cloud.py`
2. `python Tools/three_d/verify_bags_helmets_connections.py`
3. `blender -b --python Tools/three_d/verify_bags_helmets_blender.py`
4. `python Tools/three_d/verify_bags_helmets_cloud.py`

The author uses the existing unchanged build_models.py validation, rendering and GLB export. Checks include direct byte-for-byte deterministic re-export, exact source/crop/palette checks, unique canonical IDs and assigned atlas indices, finite raw accessor data and bounds/index/buffer ranges. Independent Blender 4.3.2 imports the SHA-checked files and verifies mesh counts, finite vertices, zero mesh repairs and zero actions. These are bounded file/geometry checks; they do not establish engine or live-scene fidelity.

Previews and reports live in `Tools/three_d/generated/cloud-review/bags-helmets/`. Every model has source-facing, orbit, rear-orbit, underside and composed-source comparison views. The two overview sheets group five models each.
