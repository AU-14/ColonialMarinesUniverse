# Garrison potted plant sources

9 new plants and a revision of the existing plant 6 were authored after inspecting each source frame. Shapes include branching and drooping palms, topiary, a sapling, pitcher plant, forked tree, jade evergreen, two cacti and bamboo. Pots retain distinct square/round silhouettes and palettes.

The solid-part geometry is newly authored and contributed under CC0-1.0 to the extent separately licensable. This does not relicense upstream visual designs or derivative rights. Retain the source license and attribution below. No original sprite pixels are embedded in model meshes.

One unit equals one tile. Plants use an exact modeled tabletop beneath their pivot when present and otherwise stand on the floor; source facing is fixed. Their height, rear branches, leaf depth and undersides are inferred. Leaves and rounded pots use stepped cuboids; these are neither scanned meshes nor final organic surfaces. No sway, damage or growth states are implemented.
