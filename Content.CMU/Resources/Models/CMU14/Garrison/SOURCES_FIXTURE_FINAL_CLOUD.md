# Final fixture cloud art drafts

Source checkpoint: `6e37a4d0a7d9433838c393a82c02422cb704dd5a`, TheHellFireo/CMU-Garrison-3D, Chip/garrison-3d.

## Scope and result

13 editable draft GLBs map exactly to 10 assigned physical-modeling prototype IDs. Three additional GLBs are reciprocal stable-open door partners with empty sourcePrototypes. The frozen inventory records 16 Redux and 3 classic placements of these types; these are potential static references, not verified rendered placement or live coverage.

Every pose has 15–43 supported editable parts. There are 18 original-pixel surface PNGs using only reserved fixture_final atlas slots 3139–3156; the remainder of the 40-slot reservation is unused. Source-colored volumes provide structure. Textures are confined to narrow grate/rim strips, warning bands, access indicators and small equipment labels. No whole-assembly sprite facade is used.

The source-facing, front/rear orbit, constant-scale and synthetic opposed-door/fixed-grate-neighbor previews were generated by the unchanged existing asset renderer. They are art review images. Camera/fixed-scale review does not establish source-perfect physical depth or native acceptance. No game, server, map, engine, shader or existing asset was edited, launched, published or pushed by this batch.

## Physical construction and review

- Doors are one-tile leaves with an outer +X jamb and partner toward -X; `yawOffset: 90` preserves the existing paired-door layout convention. Source East/West frames establish the broad leaf, while South/North compress the paired axis. Outer frame and threshold remain physical in open and closed poses. Height 2.73 and depth, including the retained overhead guide in the open physical draft, are inferred, matching the existing library scale convention rather than a claim of literal one-pixel height. The generic glass leaf has an actual transparent pane with no opaque plate behind it. Source West frame is the explicit review reference; four source directions are retained. Synthetic opposed pairs use original game partner convention: adjacent anchored leaves with opposite facing.

- The cryopod has a circular grounded pedestal, separate front entry, upright rear bed and seat, segmented side/rear glass, physical rim and low south-facing pipe. Seven flat glass sections approximate the curve. Source Sprite Y offset +0.5 and layer pipe Y offset -0.5 are retained in the source-composition evidence; they are not blindly copied to world XY. The pod has no invented patient. Bed construction, open shell depth and pipe depth are explicitly inferred.

- Recovery/medevac packages retain distinct original color bands and arrow/cross labels. Closed thick cases have connected feet, lid seams, hinges, latches and exterior ribs. The sentry-deployer package is a closed protective case with a genuine open carry handle. The spotlight has an actual recessed unlit aperture, folded lower bank, rails, connected feet and connector tips. All are ground-world packages, with no functional internal mechanisms.

- The folded omni sentry uses the actual Item-mode silhouette, amber/red details and connected exterior supports. Its lowest transformed mesh vertex was normalized to ground; no deployed base or operating pose was added.

- The hunter grate has 64 through-openings checked across its full thickness. Its original narrow rim/rib pixels are seated on actual load-bearing-looking members. The top deck is Z=0, fastener detail is +0.001, and the inferred underside reaches -0.07. An explicit four-tile synthetic arrangement preserves every perimeter; there is no source neighbor-smoothing contract to invent.

## Verification performed

- Exact SHA-1 Git blob identity checked for all successfully fetched source inputs at the pinned checkpoint, including full inherited prototype owners, RSI metadata/pixels and C# visualizer/default owners
- All 10 targets verified against both the Redux visual inventory and physicalModelingFamilies; all ancestors resolved with no missing parent
- Metadata direction counts, exact direction-frame hashes and alias groups recorded; no identical-direction claim is inferred from visual resemblance
- Every used crop is byte-exact RGBA with matching SHA-256 and dimensions; every non-textured RGB is present in the relevant source/default or fixed-structure source palette
- All 13 assemblies form one connected convex-mesh contact graph each; all 12 non-floor assemblies have a ground minimum within 1e-5, with the grate underside explicitly below its walking plane
- All 64 grate openings, all three stable-open door apertures, opaque-free glazing, the carry handle, recessed spotlight lens and empty cryopod entry pass sampled exact transformed-hull exclusion checks
- GLBs reproduce byte-for-byte through the unchanged exporter. Buffer lengths, accessors, bounds, finite transforms, indices and embedded PNG payloads pass bounded integrity checks
- Read-only canonical scan reports no duplicate atlas index, model ID or prototype mapping at verification time
- Blender 4.3.2 independently imports all 13 exported GLBs with meshes, without re-export
- No Khronos validator, native build/import, live-game, saved-map contact or complete-fidelity approval is claimed

The source exporter file has the already materialized single extra trailing newline relative to the pinned source; its implementation is identical. This batch does not modify it. Geometry/contact checks establish isolated draft construction, not engineering adequacy or map fit.
