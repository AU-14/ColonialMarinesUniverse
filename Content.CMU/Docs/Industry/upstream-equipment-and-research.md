RussianCM industry and equipment — English research notes
Reviewed 1 November 2026

I inspected RussianCM master at commit `0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6` (30 October 2026). The comparison baseline was CMU origin/master `8fb71c688b6dd1b159a9c2717e2ca2407cbd1b70`. This is a source investigation, not a playtest or a full fork merge audit. English explanations below summarize current mechanics; Russian labels are included so you can recognize them in-game.

The main addition is a connected colony industry progression: gathering and primitive tools → forging and casting → steam machines → powered factories → advanced supplies and trade. Most of this implementation is in Content.RUMC. CMU already has underground building and cave-in systems; RussianCM extends those with resource layers, deposits and tool requirements. Searches against CMU origin/master did not find the new pump, drill, recipe-machine and oil-system classes or the representative RUMC machine IDs inspected here. That does not imply every underlying concept or asset originated in this fork.

The water feature you mentioned is present in two forms: terrain water collection and chemical separation.

The brass and steel water pumps must stand on water. Their server code recognizes descendants of FloorWaterEntity and CMFloorShallowWaterEntity, which covers cave pools and the RMC shallow/deep-water and river family. They add water to an internal tank while correctly positioned. The pump code does not consume electrical power, steam or fuel, and does not deplete the terrain water. Brass pumps produce 2 game fluid units per second with a 50-unit tank; steel pumps produce 4 with a 100-unit tank. Actual delivered throughput is also constrained by ports and pipes. [Pump implementation](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Steam/FluidPumpSystem.cs) [Pump prototypes](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Steam/steam.yml#L239)

Water then has several uses:

- A fuel-fired boiler makes steam. Coal, coke, planks or branches supply the fire. Steam drives drills, grinders, presses, smelters and generators.
- Ore washers consume water to recover more powder from mined material.
- An electrolyzer converts 20 water into 20 hydrogen and 10 oxygen in a six-second recipe.
- A brine recipe consumes 30 table salt and 30 water and produces 15 each of chlorine, hydrogen and sodium hydroxide.
- Water also supports concrete production, agriculture, gem recovery and petroleum processing.

These are game recipe amounts, not real-world engineering units or chemical instructions. [Boiler behavior](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Steam/BoilerSystem.cs) [Electrolyzer recipes](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Electric/electric_recipes.yml#L1247)

The liquid pipes and steam pipes are separate systems. Liquid pipes hold one reagent at a time and equalize gradually with their neighbors. Screwdriver links join pipes and machine ports; longer links conduct less, with a 12-tile maximum link distance. Downward liquid-pipe variants connect levels. Steam uses the existing atmospheric water-vapor gas and gas-pipe network. [Fluid definitions](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Shared/Fluids/FluidComponents.cs) [Flow implementation](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Fluids/FluidSystem.cs)

Mining gives this industry its material supply. The fork adds ore-bearing walls and larger floor deposits for drilling. A default floor deposit holds 10,000–20,000 pieces across at most five resource types. Steam and electric rigs extract a random 1–10 pieces per eight-second cycle, using six output slots. Deposits eventually disappear when exhausted. A rig placed on compatible ordinary cave rock instead produces stone or black-stone fragments indefinitely; this does not provide unlimited ore directly. Current code no longer requires replacement drill heads. [Deposit and drill defaults](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Shared/Mining/MiningComponents.cs) [Drill execution](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Mining/OreDrillSystem.cs)

The default underground progression is:

| Depth relative to the surface | Environment | Required digging tool |
|---|---|---|
| -1 to -2 | Clay, coal/coke veins, occasional caves/oil | Shovel |
| -3 to -9 | Stone, ore/crystal veins, water pools and deposits | Pickaxe |
| -10 and deeper | Black stone, richer valuable-metal/gem distribution, lava | Jackhammer |

Maps can configure or disable the underlying vertical-building system; these are the component defaults. The powered shovel and pickaxe halve the normal pit-digging duration. The jackhammer uses a 0.35 multiplier and has the tag required to enter black stone. A battery-powered prospector reports nearby ore and oil deposits with distance/direction. [Layer definitions](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.CMU/Shared/ZLevelBuilding/ZBuildableMapComponent.cs#L141) [Powered mining tools](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Electric/electric_tools.yml#L182) [Prospecting implementation](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Electric/ElectricToolSystem.cs#L69)

Resources include iron, copper, zinc, aluminum, lead, chromium, cobalt, tungsten, silver, gold, titanium and platinum, alongside uranium veins, quartz, saltpeter, cinnabar, phosphorus-bearing crystals, other minerals and gemstones. Ore walls and floor-deposit tables have different distributions. For example, the plain stone floor-deposit table lacks platinum, but a rare platinum-bearing wall exists in the stone-layer generation list. [Floor-deposit resource tables](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Mining/mining.yml) [Ore-wall prototypes](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Production/stone_veins.yml)

Early production is manual. Trees provide branches, logs and fuel; logs can be split with an axe or sawn into planks. Cutting a tree's bark allows resin collection. Furnaces heat crucibles, molds cast molten material, and hot workpieces are shaped on an anvil using tools. There is heat/burn handling and a real strike-based forging system. Clay and stone furnace descriptions specify different heat ceilings; the implementation also makes hot-item handling significant. [Wood harvesting](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Flora/TreeSystem.cs) [Resin tapping](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Flora/TreeTappingSystem.cs) [Heating, casting and burns](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Forge/ForgeSystem.cs) [Anvil work](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Forge/AnvilSystem.cs)

Steam is the first mechanized stage. A practical chain is water pump → boiler → gas pipes → steam drill/grinder/press/smelter. A steam generator instead converts supplied steam into electricity. The steel industrial family gives an alternative to building everything from brass: the industrial generator supplies 6,000 power versus the ordinary generator's 3,000; the industrial smelter suppresses slag; the industrial extractor has double processing speed. Some industrial models add automatic item ports rather than a higher production rate. [Basic steam equipment](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Steam/steam.yml) [Industrial variants](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Steam/industrial.yml)

Ordinary steam smelting uses a mold and sufficient ore/powder for a batch of three ingots; slag accumulation can stop it until drained. The industrial version avoids that byproduct. A condenser cools supported liquids into solid output, with slag/water as one configured use. The equipment called a “condenser” should not be assumed to implement a steam-to-water recycling loop. [Steam smelting behavior](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Steam/SteamSmelterSystem.cs) [Smelting recipes](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Smelting/steam_smelter.yml) [Condenser configuration](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Steam/steam.yml#L377)

The cargo catalog offers the industry starter crate for 1,500. Its contents include a steel pump, three steel liquid pipes, a steel boiler, industrial generator/drill/press/smelter and two molds. Fuel, power distribution and the remaining steam connections still have to be arranged. It is an entry kit, not an already-running factory. Oil is a second major production chain. A powered pump draws crude from finite underground deposits into its tank. Default oil reserves are 30,000–60,000 units; the pump's nominal rate is 10 units per second. The distillation column separates crude into naphtha, light fuel, heavy fuel and refinery gas. Blending makes diesel. Cracking makes ethylene; polymerization makes plastic pellets; the bending machine presses pellets into sheets. Diesel generators and gas turbines use the resulting fuels. [Oil defaults](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Shared/Electric/OilComponents.cs) [Oil extraction/generation](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Server/Electric/OilSystem.cs) [Refinery machinery](https://github.com/flex5hybrid/RussianCM/blob/0e24ae881d565777cc2ef47f6c38d9ff1df8d4d6/Content.RUMC/Resources/Prototypes/CMU14/Electric/electric_oil.yml)

Several details matter when judging what to reuse:

- Current content has substantial implementation behind it: common recipe execution, power pauses, output-space checks, item transfer, per-pipe fluids, ore depletion and interactive tools. It is more than a set of named props.
- Old guides and descriptions can be stale. Drill heads and grinder blades were removed. The initial coke-startup problem reported in PR 522 is no longer accurately described as “there is no starting coke”: current generation explicitly places coke-bearing walls, including in clay.
- The original weapon/armor/ammunition bench additions were subsequently removed in PR 545. Do not count them as current features from an older announcement.
- The thermal centrifuge's iron recipe consumes six iron powder and returns six iron powder plus one chromium powder. Refeeding its output can therefore repeatedly create the secondary metal according to the current recipe. That is a balance concern inferred from the recipe and common execution code, not a reproduced live exploit.
- The recycler only executes defined matching recipes; the description saying it “takes any item” describes slot acceptance, not universal recovery for every possible game object.
- Porting the useful pieces would require carrying their dependencies together: deposits and generation for mining; fluid tanks/ports and steam handling for boilers; common recipes, kits, slots and automation for factories. It would also require checking integration with CMU's existing power, resource and underground systems. These dependencies are now included in the CMU import; see [import notes](import-notes.md) for integration and verification.

My assessment: the most consequential addition is a repeatable civilian production loop. Water, terrain, mining and energy supply become inputs into colony construction and equipment. The best-defined feature groups to evaluate independently are (1) pumps/boilers/steam industry, (2) mining/prospecting and ore processing, (3) oil/fuel/plastics, and (4) factory automation. Whether their balance and multiplayer behavior are ready for CMU needs a running-game check.
