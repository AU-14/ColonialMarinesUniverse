# Stable Garrison Redux: staged art batch 9

This archive contains 637 canonical draft assemblies: 462 exact types from the original missing queue, plus 80 separately audited inherited replacement types, with conditional, directional and unbound studies counted only as assemblies. A further standalone Blender thin-shell study is separate from the canonical count. The committed inventory lists 951 Redux records and 454 classic comparison records for those types. These are potential mapping records, not verified live replacements.

This cumulative batch includes the earlier medical, tools, food, vendors and dresser drafts plus source-specific fixtures, chemical/drink containers, storage cases, pizza shells, colony processor, outdoor machinery, ammunition packaging, service machines and loose equipment, remaining food, civilian outerwear, uniforms, room props, factory consoles and the final vending families, hand tools and devices, small medical props, structural vehicle props and printed/smoking props, loose headwear, additional drinks and machines, remaining utility/storage, materials/leisure and loose insignia, remaining weapon/field-gear/clothing/vehicle/fixture/decor/natural props and unbound topology studies. Source-state studies and cardinal variants are explicitly separate from target prototype coverage. One broken-grille runtime counterpart has no saved-map inventory record and is excluded from Redux progress. This archive supersedes earlier batches. All geometry remains draft. The GLB files contain 29 portable animation clips in total; other state variants are static scenes. Every model remains `draft`.
