# Fighter sound candidates

Reviewed listings on 2026-09-24. The selected flight and MANPAD recordings are
implemented and documented in [AUDIO.md](AUDIO.md). The remaining alternatives
are listing-based suggestions for auditioning; they have not been imported.

## MANPAD launch

Selected and implemented: [Missile firing fl by NHMWretched](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-missile-firing-fl-106655/).
The [original upload](https://freesound.org/people/NHMWretched/sounds/151858/)
is 4.742 seconds and CC0. Its author describes a designed missile cue made from
sonic-boom and thunder recordings. Its attack and full tail follow the existing
mechanical acquisition buildup at their original pitch.
It is a designed effect, not a field recording of a MANPAD.

Short alternative: [Rocket Launcher](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-rocket-launcher-307512/),
listed as about one second. Its duration suits a brief launch report. Only the
Pixabay listing/license has been checked for this alternative.

Keep the approved buildup free of electronic lock tones and the removed opening
metallic dinks. Do not replace it just to change the launch cue.
