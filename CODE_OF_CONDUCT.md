# CMU Code of Conduct

CMU is built by volunteers from all over the world. Developers, mappers, spriters, testers,
and people who just like talking about the game. Diversity is one of our strengths, but it
only works if everyone can participate comfortably. This code sets the ground rules.

It applies to everyone in CMU spaces at every level: commenters, contributors, and
maintainers alike. It is not an exhaustive list of forbidden behavior. Read it as a guide
to enriching the community, not a rulebook to argue against!

## Our standards

- **Be friendly and patient.** Many of us are not communicating in our first language, and
  many are contributing to a codebase like this for the first time.
- **Be welcoming.** We support people of all backgrounds and identities, including but not
  limited to race, ethnicity, national origin, immigration status, social and economic
  class, education, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity and expression, age, size,
  family status, political belief, religion, and mental/physical ability.
  An exhaustive list, but our diversity and our respect of it is the future.
- **Be considerate.** Your work is used by others and you build on theirs. Your decisions
  affect players and contributors - weigh those consequences.
- **Be respectful and assume good faith.** Disagreement is inevitable; personal attacks are not.
  Frustration never licenses hostility, though we are human and frustration clouds our judgment.
  Assume contributions are made in good faith, even ones that don't end up merged.
- **Be careful with your words.** We conduct ourselves professionally. Harassment and
  exclusionary behavior are unacceptable, including but not limited to:
  - violent threats or language directed at a person;
  - discriminatory jokes and language;
  - posting sexually explicit or violent material;
  - posting or threatening to post someone's personal information ("doxing");
  - personal insults, especially those using racist or sexist terms;
  - unwelcome sexual attention;
  - advocating or encouraging any of the above;
  - repeated harassment: if someone asks you to stop, you stop.
- **When we disagree, understand why.** CMU draws on a wide range of perspectives, and being
  unable to see why someone holds a view doesn't make them wrong! People make mistakes;
  blame doesn't fix them. Focus on resolving the issue and learning from it.

## Scope

This code applies to the CMU GitHub repositories and spaces managed by the CMU project.
Other spaces, such as the CMU Discord and game servers, have their own rules but follow the
same spirit.

## Enforcement

If you believe someone is violating this code, report it by privately contacting a CMU
code maintainer or the host(s) on the [CMU Discord](https://discord.gg/colonialmarines).

Maintainers may take corrective action on content that violates this code: editing or
deleting content, and temporarily or permanently blocking a user from the repository.
Outright offensive and bigoted content is always unacceptable; maintainers may also act on
ambiguous content that fails to be constructive - hyperbole, hostile tone, dragging
unrelated PRs into a discussion, or issues opened solely to stir conflict. Disagreeing with
a change or a person is fine when done respectfully and constructively.

If you believe an action was incorrectly applied to you, raise it with another code maintainer
on Discord.
