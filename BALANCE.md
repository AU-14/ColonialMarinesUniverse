# Balance

Every balance PR fills in the Why / Balance questions in the PR template: what goes wrong in a
round, what cmss13/RMC14 and CMU do now (every changed value before -> after), why this fix,
and what the other side can still do about it. "It's OP" or "people want it" isn't a reason by
itself, and reworks of a faction, threat, economy or objective get a feedback post on Discord
first, linked in the PR. A maintainer approves every balance PR, and never their own.
